Rhythm TD is a short Tower Defence game to challenge myself to merge two different game genres together; Mixing Rhythm games with Tower Defence.
As the player you place down the base of your towers and have to click them to the beat to build them. Along with this element of rhythm, 
you're required to press the arrow keys at the right time to fire your towers. So it's a mix of multitasking. The systems included are as follows;

Rhythm/BPM System
Save/Load System
Entity and Towers System
