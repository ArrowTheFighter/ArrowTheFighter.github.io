Architect of Evil is a Rogue Like Dungeon Builder hybrid where you play as the Dungeon Master. Inspired by Evil Genius, Dungeons 3, Slay the Spire and Dungeon Keeper.
Cast out by your fellow dungeon architects from a Mega Coporation you go off to build your own dungeon to challenge heroes.
As the player you unlock architects and different dungeon buildings to design the best fortified dungeon in an attempt to keep your gold.
As the developer of the games systems, a lot of intricate designing was required. Some of the core systems are;

Building and Grid System
Saving/Loading
Dialogue/ Narration/ Subtitles System
Relic/Artifacts System
Stats/ Entities and Upgrades System
Currency/ Purchases System
BFE and A* Pathfinding Algorithms for Hero Navigation
Behaviour Tree Designs.
