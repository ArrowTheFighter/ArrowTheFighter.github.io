Idle Cultists is a unique spin on the Incremental genre, mixing a little bit of management with the quirky design of cultists devoting themselves to an orb.
The player clicks an orb to gain devotion which unlocks different paths throughout the dungeon which unlocks as you progress.

I've been the only developer on the game, focusing primarily on expansive and extendable game designs such as;

Entities
Pickup Systems
Incremental Purchase and Upgrades
Skill Trees
Stat Systems
Global Interaction Systems
Dialogue Bark Systems
Dynamic Generic Save/Load Systems
Achievements Systems
Admin/Debug Console Panel

View the Github Page here: <https://github.com/LeGingerDev/IdleCultists>
